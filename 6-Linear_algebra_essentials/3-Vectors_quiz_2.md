# Magnitude of a vector

$$
   x =
      \begin{bmatrix}
       5 \\
       1
      \end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
   y =
      \begin{bmatrix}
       -4 \\
       -1
      \end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
||\vec{v}|| =
\sqrt{5^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{26} \approx 5.099
$$

$$
||\vec{y}|| =
\sqrt{(-4)^2 + (-1)^2} = \sqrt{17} \approx 4.123
$$

$$
\deg(v) = \arctan(1 / 5) \approx 0.197 (radian) \approx 11.28 (degrees)
$$

$$
\deg(y) = \arctan(-1 / -4) \approx 0.245 - \pi  (radian) \approx -2.90 (radian) \approx -166.16 (degrees)
$$
